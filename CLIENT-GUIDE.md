# CLIENT GUIDE — Chaitu's Naturals Shop Manager
### How to use your website (no computer knowledge needed)

Open this on your phone: **https://chaitus-naturals.pages.dev/admin**

> [Screenshot placeholder: the admin login page]

---

## 1. Log in

1. Tap the small **“Shop owner?”** text at the bottom.
2. Type your **email** and **password**, tap **Log in**.
3. If you type it wrong 5 times, wait **5 minutes** and try again.
4. If you don't touch anything for **2 hours**, you will be logged out. Just log in again.

> [Screenshot placeholder: typing email and password]

## 2. Add a new product

1. Tap **Products**, then tap **+ Add product**.
2. **Product name** — for example “Herbal Hair Oil”.
3. **Description** — write what it is and how to use it.
4. **Photos** — tap **+** and choose up to **5 photos** from your phone.
   - The **first photo** is the main photo customers see first.
   - To change the order, **hold a photo and drag it**.
   - Only JPG, PNG or WebP photos, each smaller than 5 MB.
5. **Price in Rs** — numbers only, for example `299`.
6. Tap **Add product**. Done! It now shows on the website.

> [Screenshot placeholder: filled Add product form with photos]

## 3. Put an offer (discount)

1. In **Products**, tap **Edit** on the product.
2. Turn **Offer?** to **Yes**.
3. Choose **% Percent** (for example 15) or **₹ Fixed Rs** (for example 50 off).
4. Type the number. You will see **“Customer will pay: ₹…”** — check it is correct.
5. **Offer end date** (optional): pick the last date. After that date the offer stops by itself.
6. Tap **Save changes**.

> [Screenshot placeholder: offer toggle with price preview]

## 4. Mark In Stock / Out of Stock

1. Tap **Edit** on the product.
2. Find **Stock** and tap it to change between **In Stock** and **Out of Stock**.
3. Tap **Save changes**.
4. When a product is Out of Stock, customers see a grey badge and cannot order it.

## 5. Hide a product without deleting it

1. Tap **Edit**, find **Show on website?**, set it to **Hide**, tap **Save changes**.
2. To show it again, set it back to **Show**.

To delete forever: tap **Delete** and confirm. To make a copy: tap **Copy**.

> [Screenshot placeholder: product list with Edit / Copy / Delete buttons]

## 6. Post a top banner (festival message)

1. Tap **Top banner**.
2. **Title** — short, for example “Festival Offer”.
3. **Message** — for example “From 20 Oct to 31 Oct: 15% off all hair oils.”
4. Pick **Start date** and **End date**. The banner hides by itself after the end date.
5. Tap **Add banner**. Only the **newest** banner shows at the top of the website.
6. To remove it, tap **Turn off** or **Delete**.

> [Screenshot placeholder: banner form and banner list]

## 7. Approve customer reviews

1. Tap **Reviews**.
2. New reviews come under **Pending**. Read each one.
3. Tap **Approve** to show it on the website, **Reject** to keep it hidden, **Delete** to remove it forever.

> [Screenshot placeholder: pending reviews with Approve / Reject / Delete]

## 8. Change your password

1. Tap **Password**.
2. Type a new password (at least 8 letters/numbers).
3. Tap **Save new password**.

---

**Need help?** Your website helper can reset your password from the Supabase Dashboard (Authentication → Users). Keep your password private and never share it.
